AWAN VALLEY RING RUN

Fly the ring course, mind your fuel, bring her home.

Eight glowing gates wind across the valley — over the lake, through the mountain gap, and home. Each gate pays 100 points and a splash of fuel. Fly them all, then put her down gently on the runway. Hit the ground hard, ditch in the lake, or run dry, and the sortie is over.
